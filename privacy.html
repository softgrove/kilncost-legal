Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026

Local calculation data, limited anonymous usage data. Everything you enter — kilns, unit prices, firing conditions, history, and presets — stays on your device and is never sent to us. KilnCost sends limited anonymous usage events to a server operated by the developer. There is no account, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Who we are

KilnCost (“the app”, “we”) is provided by Softgrove, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: support@softgrove.app.

What data the app handles

You may enter kilns, unit prices, firing conditions, calculation history, and presets (“your data”). Your data is stored only locally on your device. We never receive this data, and the app does not provide account sync or a cloud backup service.

No calculation content is sent. This includes kiln names, unit prices, firing conditions, history, and presets.

Anonymous usage analytics

KilnCost sends events for app launches, running a firing-cost calculation, reaching an export or save limit as a non-premium user (share card, preset save, history CSV), Lifetime offer views, purchase-related actions (purchase start, completion, and failure with a fixed reason), and restoring a purchase. Each event contains the app version, a timestamp, the event name, and, when applicable, a paywall trigger or product SKU.

These events do not include any identifier: there is no UUID, account ID, advertising identifier (IDFA), or device identifier (IDFV) generated, stored, or sent. KilnCost does not access IDFA or IDFV. The events cannot be linked back to a specific device or person; the developer can only see aggregate counts.

We use these events only to understand which features are used and where people stop in the app flow so we can improve KilnCost. We do not use them for advertising or tracking.

Export, sharing, and deletion

  • Export / Share: You can export a share card image or a history CSV file and share it using the system share sheet. Exported files are under your control once you share them; nothing is sent to us. Non-premium accounts are limited on some export and save actions; reaching that limit is what triggers the export-gate event described above.
  • Delete: You can delete any entry at any time. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Purchases and payments

KilnCost offers an optional Lifetime unlock as a one-time purchase, sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Your purchase entitlement is verified on-device through Apple’s StoreKit framework.

Network connections

The calculation and history features work fully offline. When a network is available, KilnCost may send the anonymous usage events described above to a server operated by the developer. Apple’s system frameworks also communicate with the App Store for purchases and purchase restoration.

Disclaimer

Cost and time estimates produced by KilnCost are approximations. Your actual costs depend on your electricity contract, rate plan, and the condition of your kiln. KilnCost does not provide electrical work or safety advice; consult a qualified professional for kiln installation, wiring, or maintenance.

Children

KilnCost is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

KilnCost has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Anonymous usage events are sent only to the developer’s own server. We do not sell or provide these events to third parties, use them for advertising, or use them to track you across apps or websites.
